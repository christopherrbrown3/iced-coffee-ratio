# Visual critique: calculator shell

Method: dual-agent Assessment A/B. Assessment A performed visual judgment at 320 px, 390 px, and 1440 px. Assessment B independently ran the detector and captured browser/DOM evidence before synthesis. Questions were skipped because the findings aligned directly with the user's requested changes.

## Baseline

- Nielsen score: 28/40
- Cognitive-load failures: 1/8 (visual hierarchy)
- Detector findings: 0
- Horizontal overflow: 0 px at phone and desktop evidence sizes

## Prioritized findings

1. **P1 — Product name obscures the utility.** Replace Ratio Hero everywhere with Iced Coffee Calculator, including document/PWA metadata, accessible names, clipboard copy, and documentation.
2. **P1 — Preset state can become untruthful.** Persist the active recipe and its method, migrate legacy settings, and make edits resolve to a truthful Custom state. Recipe data must own compatibility and timer behavior.
3. **P1 — Focus and live-region behavior need correction.** Use a two-color visible focus treatment, keep the ticking clock out of live announcements, expose units and slider consequences, and manage focus across calculator/timer views.
4. **P2 — The ice mascot is too small and competes with a second illustration system.** Make the mascot the sole protagonist in the masthead, Brew payoff, and timer.
5. **P2 — The native slider is visually unfinished and semantically vague.** Retain native range semantics while adding a thick inked track, flat progress fill, printed ticks, a large thumb, exact endpoints, and a live hot-water/ice summary.

## Implementation disposition

All five findings were accepted. The updated design also strengthens the Brew payoff, adds researched method-specific presets without overloading the Pick panel, and treats the V60 as flow-through instead of assigning a fabricated capacity.
